THE PIPELINE

Three stages, no shortcuts

Most "custom" models fine-tune something that already knows language. Mango-LLM didn't have that luxury — it learned to read before it learned to write.

01

Tokenize

A byte-pair encoder learns the vocabulary from the training text itself — no borrowed dictionary, no existing tokenizer.

8,000-token vocabulary
02

Attend

A causal transformer — self-attention, feed-forward blocks, positional encoding — assembled layer by layer from first principles.

Multi-head causal attention
03

Generate

Trained on children's short stories until it could hold a character's name, a plot, and an ending across a full page.

Trained on TinyStories

Nothing to hide behind

No GPT weights. No fine-tuning shortcut. Every parameter started random and was shaped entirely by training — which is also why it only knows how to do one thing well.

HONEST BENCHMARK

Measured against GPT-2, on the same text

Both models were tested on the exact same held-out stories, each using its own tokenizer. Lower is better.

Mango-LLM (110k steps) 4.12 perplexity
GPT-2 Medium (355M, zero-shot) 8.80 perplexity

A model 1.7× smaller, specialized on this domain, out-predicting a general-purpose model on its home turf.
